_Comprehensive Range of Interior and Exterior Fixtures Enable Clients to Experience the Full Potential of Light_

SOLON, Ohio, Feb. 13, 2025 /PRNewswire/ -- [Kichler Lighting LLC](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4362732-1&h=1937376075&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.kichler.com%2F&a=Kichler+Lighting+LLC), a leader in residential lighting and ceiling fans, is returning to Las Vegas for Design and Construction Week, this time making its highly anticipated debut as part of the 2025 Kitchen & Bath Industry Show (KBIS), February 25-27. Showcasing thoughtfully designed collections, Kichler celebrates the power of lighting to bring effect, emotion and life to a space.

[Continue Reading](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/kichler-lighting-to-exhibit-at-2025-kbis-with-curated-collections-designed-to-inspire-spaces-302376606.html#continue-jump)

Rooted in the belief that the right lighting transforms a house into a home, Kichler's latest collections seamlessly blend organic-inspired materials, timeless aesthetics and modern technology to create warm, inviting spaces.

"Lighting has the ability to not only add beauty to a space through its design aesthetics but also to impact how a space feels and functions throughout the day," said Brian Nobbe, Executive Vice President of Marketing for Coleto Brands, the newly formed parent company for Kichler Lighting. "Our newest products deliver captivating designs and provide semi-customization opportunities to ensure the best light for the space, whether that's through finish and material choices, installation options, adjustability or color temperature selection."

KBIS visitors will experience a curated selection of lighting solutions that enhance both indoor and outdoor spaces, combining artistry with cutting-edge technology. With a focus on elevating everyday moments, Kichler's 2025 collections feature a mix of statement fixtures and refined accents.

Purposefully designed kitchen and bath vignettes illustrate the art of light layering through a combination of decorative and architectural lighting. These elevated spaces – featuring Fabuwood® cabinetry, Cosentino® surfaces, Delta® decorative plumbing and accessories, and Kraus® kitchen sink – are intended to showcase the subtle, yet meaningful impact lighting can have in these hard-working spaces.

Attendees are invited to visit [Booth N1526](https://c212.net/c/link/?t=0&l=en&o=4362732-1&h=1819782036&u=https%3A%2F%2Fkbis.a2zinc.net%2Fkbis2025%2FPublic%2Feventmap.aspx%3FEventId%3D1939%26MapItBoothID%3D1761024%26MapItBooth%3DN1526%26MapID%3D1282&a=Booth+N1526) to immerse themselves in Kichler's debut firsthand and discover lighting that blends function, beauty and a deep appreciation for the way we gather.
